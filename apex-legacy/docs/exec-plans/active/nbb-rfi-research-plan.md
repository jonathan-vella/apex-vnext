# NBB Cloud DR RFI: Documentation and Diagram Research Plan

> Generated by Copilot agent | 2026-11-20

**Status:** Draft for review. This plan does not report new findings or authorize architecture changes, external
sharing, or a recovery drill.

## Objective

Determine what should stay, change, or be qualified in the NBB v2 advisory response and its diagrams. Produce an
evidence-backed list of changes that a partner can use to answer the RFI, explain the recovery design, and identify
what NBB, Microsoft, the partner, carriers, or specialist vendors still have to prove.

Treat these as the existing v2 baseline, not new findings: separate **at-most-two-hour failover** and
**at-most-two-hour planned failback** windows, predictability ahead of standby savings, VMware to AVS and back,
Nutanix to NC2 and back, strict one-to-one database and Kubernetes parity, and zero Azure Site Recovery (ASR).
Compare the existing `R1` Gulf-plus-EU and `R2` Frankfurt-consolidated options (plus the `R3` Single-GCC contingency)
without declaring any option approved. Record evidence that challenges the baseline as a decision for review rather
than silently changing the design.

## Evidence, sequence, and exit criteria

1. **Freeze the baseline.** Inventory the response, diagrams, and workbook inputs. Assign each research item the
   requirement/worksheet or decision it supports and identify its audience. Separate current claims from questions.
2. **Research the design blockers first.** Prioritize reverse RDS replication (incl. `App 40`'s `~20 TB` PostgreSQL
   and `App 17`–`App 18` PostgreSQL `18.3` version check), `App 40` analytics failback semantics, EKS persistent data
   written during DR, AVS/NC2 capacity and reprotection, and written AIX/Solaris vendor support. Run regional/transit
   checks in parallel where they affect those paths. Use Microsoft Learn and product support matrices for Azure
   claims, CBB publications and NBB compliance for regulatory interpretation, and vendor or carrier confirmation for
   entitlements, certified configurations, circuit behavior, and capacity. A public SKU listing does not prove capacity.
3. **Record and reconcile evidence.** For each material claim record its source title/link or restricted reference,
   date, relevant version/region, applicable topology, status, owner, and next action. Use statuses such as
   source-verified, customer-reported, vendor confirmation required, and drill unproven. Flag contradictions
   rather than selecting whichever source supports a preferred architecture.
4. **Specify what would prove recovery.** Use the response's failover clock definition and confirm the declaration
   boundary with NBB; capture incident detection separately and finish only when agreed services pass business
   checks. Start planned failback at application ingress quiesce after reverse sync and health gates; finish when
   service and agreed test writes are verified at the original site. Record pre-window convergence time separately.
   Measure RPO at the latest consistent recoverable point for each tier; do not infer it from the two-hour windows.
5. **Review the proposed changes before editing the package.** Require a traceability row for each in-scope
   requirement and inventory sheet, an owner and next step for every material unknown, and a diagram-to-text
   consistency checklist. Obtain agreement on the edits and what can be shared. Keep internal plans under
   `docs/exec-plans/active/` so partner archives stay clean; no diagram, ROM, BoQ, deck, or archive regeneration and
   no live test belong to the initial plan-writing step.
